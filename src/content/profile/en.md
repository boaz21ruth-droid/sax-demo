Vann picked up the alto at eleven, in a school marching band that owned four
saxophones and shared them between nine players. Twenty years on, he has played
more than 900 shows in twelve countries, from festival main stages to
forty-seat clubs where the front row can read the engraving on the bell.

His sound sits between soul-jazz and pop: a big, warm tenor for ballads, a
bright alto that carries over a dance floor. He leads a quartet, works as a
featured soloist with DJs and orchestras, and arranges well-known songs so a
wedding crowd and a jazz crowd both recognise them.

Off stage he teaches. About thirty students, aged nine to sixty-four, take
weekly lessons in person and online.
